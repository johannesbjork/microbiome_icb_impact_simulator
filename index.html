Decision model

Current pathway

Cancer patient eligible for ICB

A substantial fraction benefits, but many patients still experience primary non-response.

Lever 1 — Predict

Use a microbiome-based companion diagnostic to estimate ICB response probability before treatment.

Lever 2 — Optimize

Use FMT or another microbiome-directed intervention to increase response among patients predicted not to benefit.

Lever 3 — Prevent microbiome disruption

Where clinically appropriate, reduce unnecessary antibiotic and PPI exposure that may compromise the microbiome during ICB. The magnitude of recoverable response is uncertain and is therefore treated as a scenario parameter.
